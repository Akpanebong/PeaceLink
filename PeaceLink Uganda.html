What you can do on PeaceLink

Three tools that help refugee and host community members build working relationships and settle differences early.

Trade

Post or find offers for farm produce, livestock, firewood, tools, and services, and meet buyers and sellers from both communities.

  • Choose your settlement or district, and your village or zone
  • Share only what a buyer needs to contact you

Agreements

Record what communities have agreed on, so everyone has the same understanding later.

  • Land use and grazing arrangements
  • Water points and firewood collection
  • Market access and shared facilities
  • Parties, witnesses, and local leaders who endorsed the agreement

Resolve

Report a concern or dispute and follow it through ADR, from first report to outcome.

  • Disputes over land, natural resources, water, crop damage, and trade
  • Referral to Local Council courts, settlement management, and protection partners when a case needs more than mediation
  • Serious cases, such as gender-based violence or child protection concerns, go to the right service and are not handled on the platform
